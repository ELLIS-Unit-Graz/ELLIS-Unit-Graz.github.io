# ELLIS Sites NeurIPS Fest 2026 & Cookies with GraML

{% include figure.html image="images/2026cookies.png" width="400px" %}

On December 10, 2026, we will have a joint event together with the Graz Center for Machine Learning [(GraML)](https://www.tugraz.at/en/research/research-at-tu-graz/research-centers/graz-center-for-machine-learning/): the [ELLIS Sites NeurIPS Fest 2026: Celebrate, Connect, Collaborate](https://ellis.eu/events/ellis-sites-neurips-fest-2026) and the **Cookies with GraML** mini conference.  

We will have 7 Lightning talks on "AI for Science" and a poster session. 

# Program

Date: Thursday December 10, 2026

 9:00 -  9:15 Opening & Updates from GraML
 9:15 - 11:00 Short Presentations on "AI for Science"
11:00 - 13:30 Poster Session & Cookies

Location: Inffeldgasse 16 b, HSi11 & Foyer
